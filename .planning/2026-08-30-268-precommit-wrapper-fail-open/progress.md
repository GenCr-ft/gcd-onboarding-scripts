### The recursion is reproduced, not inferred

AC-5 returned **exit 124** — `timeout` killed it. The wrapper genuinely called itself unbounded,
in a test, from the precondition observed live in `gencr-ft.github.io`: our wrapper sitting at
`pre-commit.legacy`. The plan reported this as measured at 7 levels; the arm now pins it.

### AC-9 passes in red, for a reason that changes after the fix

Pre-change, a re-run *is* skipped — by the version-less skip branch. So idempotence holds
accidentally. After the fix it must still hold, but for the right reason: a **version match**. Its
validity therefore comes from mutation, not from the red run, which is why it is a guard and not
evidence.

### Fixture safety

The generator iterates children of `GFT_PROJECTS_HOME` containing `.git` and writes into their
`.git/hooks`. Every arm stages its own workspace under a `mktemp -d` `HOME` with a trap-cleanup; an
arm that forgot to override `GFT_PROJECTS_HOME` would deploy hooks into the developer's real clones.
That is the most dangerous thing in this work item and it is handled at the harness level, once.
